# Centrifugal Pump Maintenance Manual

## Low discharge pressure

If discharge pressure drops below normal, inspect the suction line for blockage, confirm the suction valve is fully open, and check inlet pressure. Do not run the pump dry.

## High bearing temperature

If bearing temperature exceeds 80 C, stop the equipment and inspect lubrication level, bearing wear, and shaft alignment. Allow the equipment to cool before inspection.

## Mechanical seal leakage

For leakage around the mechanical seal, isolate electrical power, close the valves, and release internal pressure before replacing the seal. Maintenance requires lockout-tagout and a qualified technician.
